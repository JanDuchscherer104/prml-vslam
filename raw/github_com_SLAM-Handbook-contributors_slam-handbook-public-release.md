This book will cover the theoretical background of SLAM, its applications, and its future as spatial AI. We expect this handbook will be a good guideline for those working in this field. Starting from the release of Part 1 in November 2024, Part 2 in March 2025, and Part 3 in May 2025, we have incrementally released the book to accommodate feedback from the public. Please use this repository's issue and discussion boards to report any issues or ideas regarding this book. How to Cite this Book? Please cite it as a book chapter.
